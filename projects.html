Featured Engineering Case Studies

Advanced hardware solutions engineered for high performance, reliability, and precision control.

Wireless & IoT

TI CC2652P7 Bluetooth-Enabled MCU Stamp Board

Compact form-factor hardware module designed for seamless multi-protocol wireless integration into broader carrier systems.

Core Architecture: Built around the Texas Instruments CC2652P7 high-performance multi-protocol wireless microcontroller supporting Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), Thread, and Zigbee.

Engineering Highlights: Ultra-low power consumption profile, optimized RF trace antenna layout for maximum signal range, and castellated stamp-hole pad configuration for robust surface-mount integration.

Data Acquisition & Power

Multi-Channel Pressure Data Acquisition Device

Handheld or field-deployed data logging unit engineered for high-precision pressure telemetry and flexible bus topology.

Core Architecture: Integrated single-cell Li-ion battery charger with hardware-level fuel gauging, USB-C compliance for power delivery and data sync, and high-contrast 7-segment LED status indicators.

Engineering Highlights: Features wireless BLE telemetry alongside a physical-layer daisy-chain topology, allowing multiple slave acquisition modules to link reliably with a master controller unit.

Medical & Embedded Control

STM32H750X Custom Therapy Device Controller

High-speed, real-time embedded control system designed for specialized pneumatic therapy equipment.

Core Architecture: Powered by the high-performance STM32H750X ARM Cortex-M7 MCU, augmented with external SDRAM memory expansion and a high-resolution RGB LCD graphic user interface.

Engineering Highlights: Real-time pressure sensor data reading, closed-loop proportional solenoid valve control, and custom high-efficiency pump driver circuitry for precise pneumatic pressure regulation.

Industrial Automation

Ruggedized Industrial IoT Gateway & Enclosure

Harsh-environment monitoring hub integrating multi-sensor acquisition with cloud-connected cellular and LoRaWAN backhaul.

Core Architecture: Wide-input DC-DC power regulation (9V-36V protection), transient voltage suppression (TVS), and a custom-molded 3D CAD polymer enclosure designed for DIN-rail mounting.

Engineering Highlights: Thermal management simulations to prevent hot spots inside sealed enclosures, IP65 ingress protection validation, and robust EMI/EMC filtering for heavy industrial floors.

Consumer Hardware

Compact Wearable Health Monitor Prototype

Miniaturized biometrics tracker balancing strict space constraints with ultra-low power longevity.

Core Architecture: Rigid-flex multi-layer PCB design combining optical sensor front-ends with an ultra-low power ARM Cortex-M4 core and internal coin-cell battery management.

Engineering Highlights: Ergonomic industrial casing design optimized for skin-contact comfort, drop-resistance modeling, and ultra-compact surface-mount component packing density.
