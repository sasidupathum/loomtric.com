About LOOMTRIC PVT LTD

Pioneering smart electronic systems and mechanical enclosures from concept to production.

Who We Are

LOOMTRIC PVT LTD is a specialized engineering firm focused on turning complex hardware concepts into market-ready products. We bridge the critical gap between electrical circuit design and physical mechanical enclosure engineering, ensuring every product we build is optimized for performance, user experience, and mass manufacturing.

Operating with a modern, agile workflow, we collaborate closely with startups, innovators, and industrial clients worldwide to bring robust hardware solutions to life.

Our Core Philosophy

We believe that great hardware is born at the intersection of electrical precision and mechanical durability. By designing for manufacturing (DFM) from day one, we help our partners minimize risks, reduce Bill of Materials (BOM) costs, and scale efficiently.
